# No snooze; escalate the Dismiss Challenge instead

**Status**: accepted

A snooze button is the single moment of weakest willpower in the morning, and the behaviour it enables — going back to sleep — is exactly what this app exists to prevent. So the product has no Snooze at all, not even a limited one. The pressure that a snooze interval would release is instead applied through Escalation: the longer an Alarm has been ringing and the more answers the owner gets wrong, the harder the Dismiss Challenge becomes.

## Consequences

- Removing Snooze raises the stakes on dismissal succeeding on the first try, which is why the Escape Hatch exists as a safety valve for defects rather than convenience.
- Escalation becomes the primary difficulty mechanism, so the challenge generator must accept a difficulty input rather than a fixed setting.
